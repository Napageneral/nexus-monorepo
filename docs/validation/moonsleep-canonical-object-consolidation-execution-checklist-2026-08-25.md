## Execution order

This is consolidation work, not another pilot.

1. **Product-to-PO family:** replace the old registry declarations and aliases;
   publish Product, Product Specification, BOM/BOM Line, Component/Variant,
   Material Specification, Supplier Quote/Line, Purchase Plan, Purchase Order,
   and PO Line; use `SWRC26004` as the first ordinary readback; migrate the
   complete family and delete its legacy surfaces.
2. **Manufacturing and quality:** Inventory Lot, Manufacturing Run/Component,
   Quality Inspection, Sample Article, Product Experiment, and quality or
   reconciliation cases.
3. **Movement and receipt:** Supply Shipment, Joint Cargo Plan, Interfacility
   Transfer, Facility Receipt, and their Record/Fact/Observation inputs.
4. **Native-owner convergence:** Entity, Contact, Place, Facets, Loop,
   Commitment, Finance, Claims, and Fulfillment references move to their native
   owners.
5. **Evidence/projection extinction:** after all real consumers are canonical,
   remove the duplicated evidence/adoption/projection tables, views, functions,
   triggers, builders, workers, timers, aliases, prompts, and current docs.
6. **Zero audit:** ordinary `rg`, PostgreSQL catalogs, deployed units, APIs,
   jobs, prompts, and business SQL must show no executable superseded language
   or storage.

## Definition of complete

The track is complete only when:

- all 24 reviewed object types have been handled in their required business
  families;
- canonical SQL answers the approved business questions;
- all required historical data is represented through canonical objects,
  native owners, Records, Facts, and accepted Observations;
- every row above is checked or explicitly retained for a legitimate
  non-duplicative owner responsibility;
- no accepted runtime alias or compatibility branch remains;
- no superseded active table, view, function, trigger, worker, timer, API,
  prompt, or current document remains.

No signed packet, custom digest protocol, candidate selector, per-object
cleanroom, or bespoke release gate is required to complete this checklist.
