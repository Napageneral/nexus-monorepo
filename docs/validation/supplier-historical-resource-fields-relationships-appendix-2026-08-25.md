## Extraction contract

- Inputs are the exact latest `SGD-0001` through `SGD-0017` bundle files listed below.
- For compact chapter bundles, observed fields are the union of `resources[].attributes` keys. For foundation-ready bundles, they are the union of `resource_rows[].row` keys.
- Outbound relationships are the union of `relationship_type` values where the resource type is `from_resource_type`, using `relationships` or `resource_relationship_projections` according to the bundle envelope.
- Names are reproduced exactly as observed. This appendix performs no normalization, inference, compatibility mapping, or schema recommendation.
- A dash means the corpus contains no outbound relationship from that resource type. Incoming relationships are intentionally outside this appendix.
