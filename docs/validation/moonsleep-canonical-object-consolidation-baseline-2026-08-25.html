Locked v2 decisions

These are the semantic consequences incorporated from the historical-ingestion review. Object-by-object field review remains part of each slice.

One complete catalog. Then family-by-family consolidation.

The vocabulary lands once. Each business family then publishes, proves, cuts over, and deletes its own legacy surfaces without holding a program-wide release open.

01 · Declare complete catalog

All 24 projected types and required owner-native addresses become uniformly targetable in one ordinary release.

02 · Publish one family

Real accepted-Observation-backed objects prove SQL, history, resolver and workflow.

03 · Cut over the family

Migrate bounded history, switch its producer and consumers, and remove temporary comparison reads.

04 · Delete family legacy

Remove aliases, old code, schemas, prompts, docs and eligible physical storage for that family.

05 · Zero audit

After all families close, verify no executable superseded vocabulary or storage remains.

Close the logical sliceAfter transaction five, perform the production business readback and zero-active-legacy census. No deployment, lock, rollback marker or bespoke proof packet spans the whole slice.
Complexity ceilingUse normal tests, one disposable PostgreSQL migration test, SQL parity, caller census, standard backup/rollback and production readback. Do not create signed migration packets, custom digest protocols, selector systems, per-object cleanrooms, a bespoke registry-activation framework, cross-track gates or bespoke receipt hierarchies.
